Narrative layer. Executives are invented and do not change the game's simulation. They follow the same logic as the coaches: ratings that rise and fall (draft eye, cap, trades, development), a reputation, a retirement age, a style the owner may like or not, assistant GMs who are the natural candidates for the top job, dismissals weighed by the owner's patience, and candidates who may refuse a seat or a team. A GM is judged from the real save: the win share of the last three seasons and how the players his team drafted in the last four classes compare with what their round usually gives.
