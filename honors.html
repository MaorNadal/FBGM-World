Division champion: first in the division by record under the league's tiebreakers. Conference champion (record): best record of the conference. Conference champion (playoffs): because this league picks its playoff field from the whole league there is no conference bracket, so this is the team of the conference that went furthest in the playoffs (most playoff wins). Super Bowl: the league's championship game, the last playoff game; its winner is the league champion. It has its own page with the history of every final. These are our own derivations from the games; the game itself may not store them.
